# SF Bike Share dataset changelog: V2 to V3

**Prepared by Carlo | 18 November 2026 | Data handoff and proposed cost-model alignment**

## 1. Purpose and scope

V3 prepares the additional data inputs requested by Joohee, Fu Yan and Jason while keeping the approved pilot unchanged: **34 San Francisco stations, 9 February-19 April 2015, hourly resolution**. The existing eight training weeks and two exploratory validation weeks are retained. Later policy-evaluation dates remain to be agreed.

The main station-hour table still has **57,120 rows** and now has **51 columns**, compared with 21 in V2. Related station-day, OD, transit, distance and parameter inputs are held in separate tables so their different units and grains remain clear. There are 21 tables in the local V3 database.

Original source files, the original analytical database and the V2 Parquet have been preserved. This report and the group message were prepared locally for manual sharing. The shared Google Doc has not been edited.

## 5. Cost-formula changes and current proposal

The detailed formula had already been expanded to address Fu Yan's multi-stop concern. The latest update converted its working units to metric and added an explicit Stage 1 approximation to match Joohee's proposed two-stage structure. These equations are proposals for formulation review; numerical cost parameters have not been calibrated or approved.

**Stage 1: allocation-cost approximation**

`C1_estimated = sum_p(F_p * z_p) + sum_t sum_(i != j)[h_t + k_t * d_ij / Lbar_t] * x_ijt`

Here `x_ijt` is bikes relocated from i to j in hour t, `h_t` is loading-plus-unloading cost in USD/bike, `k_t` is variable truck travel cost in USD/km, `d_ij` is directed road distance in km, and `Lbar_t` is a strictly positive assumed average load in bikes. `F_p * z_p` charges activation once for its defined billing period. A daily hire fee must not be charged every hour. Movements must be linked to activation and a justified throughput limit.

**Stage 2: detailed route-cost accounting**

`C2_estimated = sum_activated_vehicle_periods[F * z + (W + V) * H + P * G + M * D + A]`

`H = driving_hours + stop_setup_hours + (t_load * N_loaded + t_unload * N_unloaded) / 60 + other_paid_hours`

`G = D * q / 100`

`W` and `V` are non-overlapping crew/vehicle hourly costs; `P` is USD/litre; `G` is litres; `M` is additional USD/km; `D` is the complete route distance in km; `A` is direct fees; and `q` is litres/100 km. H and D include all relevant tours in the billing period, including required depot legs. Fixed hire is counted once per activated billing period, and handling labor is not added again if it is already in H.

Compare Stage 2's estimated route cost with Stage 1's estimated cost for the same executable movements and accounting scope. **Do not add the two estimates together.** Route order alone does not establish onboard-load, station-stock, time or depot feasibility. If the route cannot execute the proposed allocation, revise the movements or report that feasibility gap. Neither estimate is an observed operator bill, and the sequential method does not establish joint global optimality.

## 6. Validation and material findings

- **43 reconciliation checks passed**, plus **18 export row-count readbacks**. Additional independent checks verified release hashes, rider totals/splits, database reopening, road/fuel conversions and the metric weather schema.
- The hourly trip totals and original availability exposure reconcile with V2; its file checksum is unchanged.
- All 2,380 midnight opening stocks are fresh, but 67 have fewer effective bikes-plus-free-docks than the listed dock count. All stations have some discrepancy; their modal and maximum valid totals equal their listed docks. No capacity overrides were invented.
- The 34 stale hourly openings occur at 03:00 on 8 March 2015 around the daylight-saving transition. They remain missing/flagged rather than being filled with future observations.
- The unchanged 63,974 endpoint-relevant journeys are supplemented by one flagged December-to-June trip spanning the entire pilot. It remains visible for transit/outlier sensitivity, not treated as proof of continuous bike use. Long-trip and duration-mismatch flags are retained.
- Historical station inventories reflect the operations that actually occurred. They can initialize common policy experiments, but cannot be replayed as the counterfactual no-rebalancing stock path.
